# Lab - Document Enterprise Cybersecurity Issues

## 📖 Overview
This lab focuses on identifying enterprise cybersecurity risks by analyzing the assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and mitigation strategies for a fictional company, **Athena Learning Incorporated**. The exercise demonstrates how cybersecurity professionals assess and protect organizational resources using a risk-based approach.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Assess Athena's cybersecurity issues.
- Identify information, software, physical, and network assets.
- Analyze potential threats to each asset.
- Recommend appropriate mitigation techniques.
- Understand the relationship between threats and vulnerabilities.

---

## 🏢 Scenario
Athena Learning Incorporated is a global educational service provider that develops and hosts online learning content while managing student records, partner content, payment information, and cloud-based learning services. The organization operates Windows, Linux, and Apple devices across multiple international offices and supports remote work, BYOD, cloud services, and secure partner access, making cybersecurity a critical business requirement.

## 📚 Key Learning Outcomes
- Differentiate between **threats** and **vulnerabilities**.
- Classify enterprise assets based on business value.
- Identify cybersecurity risks affecting different asset types.
- Recommend layered security controls to reduce organizational risk.
- Apply enterprise cybersecurity concepts to a real-world business scenario.

---

## 📝 Reflection
This lab reinforced the importance of identifying critical assets before performing risk assessments. By mapping threats to organizational assets and implementing appropriate mitigation strategies, organizations can strengthen the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA) of their systems and data.
